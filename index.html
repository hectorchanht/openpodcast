What it is

A standalone flagship and the school’s voice. Every canon asset becomes listenable: books (OpenBook), stories (OpenStory), cards, briefings — rendered in the listener’s native tongue and local accent, with OpenPronounce resolving polyphonic readings from human-verified data. Its asset core is the Script Ocean: every piece of content exists as a script — text, structured, labelled, timed — and scripts compound: re-voiced in every language, re-cut for OpenShort, re-taught by OpenTeacher, forever, at marginal cost approaching zero. Text is the master; voices are renderings.

END GOAL

Free for everyone, forever as the base — the founding vow and the Abundance Loop: free out, real human curated data and K factor back. The metered layer is credits: premium voices, speed, personal shelves, creator tools. The proof already shipped on day one: five Cantonese episodes of The Secret, transcripts aligned word-for-word, approved by the founder’s ear in five seconds.
